
Project Title
Candidate Job-Change Intent Analysis

Organization
R&D TalentBridge

Project Overview
This project analyzes candidate data to understand how job-change intent varies across experience, education, company size, relevant experience, and city development levels. The analysis uses Excel, Power Query, PivotTables, charts, and dashboard slicers.

Business Objective
Identify candidate groups with higher or lower job-change intent to help HR teams understand workforce patterns and guide further investigation.

Dataset
The project uses the publicly sourced HR Analytics: Job Change in Data dataset. The analysis workbook contains 19,093 cleaned records. 65 records with missing experience were excluded from the analysis.

Key Findings
• Overall job-change intent rate: 24.90% (4,754 of 19,093 candidates).
• Intent rate decreases across experience bands, from 34.31% for 0–5 years to 15.31% for 21+ years.
• Candidates without relevant experience show a 33.84% intent rate, compared with 21.42% for candidates with relevant experience.
• The Company Size field is not provided for 5,915 candidates (31.0%); this missing information should not be treated as a company-size group.
• City development levels show different intent rates; these are associations and do not establish cause.

Tools and Methods
Microsoft Excel, Power Query, PivotTables, PivotCharts, slicers, and data-quality checks.

Limitations
The results describe associations in this dataset and do not prove causation or predict actual resignations. “Target = 1” represents stated job-change intent, not confirmed attrition. Missing information and the exclusion of 65 records may affect interpretation.
