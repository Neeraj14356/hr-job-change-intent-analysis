
Project: Candidate Job-Change Intent Analysis
Organization: R&D TalentBridge

1. Candidates in View
Definition: Number of candidate records included in the current analysis.
Calculation: Count of unique enrollee_id values after data cleaning.
Current total: 19,093
Note: Slicers may change this count if connected to the KPI.

2. Job-Change Intent Rate
Definition: Share of candidates with Target = 1 in the current selection.
Calculation: Candidates with Target = 1 ÷ Candidates in View.
Overall value: 24.90% (4,754 of 19,093)
Interpretation: Measures stated job-change intent in the dataset; it is not a confirmed resignation or attrition rate.

3. Candidates Seeking a Job Change
Definition: Number of candidates with Target = 1 in the current selection.
Calculation: Count of records where Target = 1.
Overall value: 4,754
Note: Slicers may change this count if connected to the KPI.

4. Company Size Not Provided
Definition: Share of candidates whose company_size value is “Not Provided.”
Calculation: Count of “Not Provided” company_size records ÷ total candidate records in the current selection.
Overall value: 31.0% (5,915 of 19,093)
Interpretation: This KPI represents missing company-size information, not a company-size category or business segment.

Data Notes
• Target = 0 means the candidate is not seeking a job change; Target = 1 means the candidate is seeking a job change.
• The analysis includes 19,093 records. 65 records with missing experience were excluded.
• Findings describe associations in this dataset and do not establish cause and effect.
• “Not Provided” indicates missing information and should be interpreted cautiously.
